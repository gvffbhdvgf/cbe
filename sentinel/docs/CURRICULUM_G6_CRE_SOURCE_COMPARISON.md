## Decision

Retain both official KICD PDFs as separate, versioned source records. Treat the October 2024 **Revised 2024** PDF as the current source candidate for future validation. Keep the June 2024 PDF for historical comparison; do not merge its headings into the revised record or ingest its learning-outcome body as current content.

Both records remain `review_status=pending` and `rights_status=not_assessed`. Neither is approved for outcome ingestion.

## Data and governance implications

1. Keep the two document codes and their source-node rows separate. A revision is a distinct source version, not an in-place overwrite.
2. Resolve the KICD catalogue-to-PDF link for the revised source and record the verification evidence.
3. Complete rights review before extracting substantive curriculum text.
4. Have a curriculum specialist validate the six differences against the source PDFs. Do not infer that a wording change alone changes the intended competency.
5. When outcome ingestion is approved, link each outcome to its document version, source page, extraction status and reviewer decision.
6. Preserve the June record as historical provenance so existing references remain reproducible.

## Current validation boundary

The comparison is generated from the staged source-heading records in the Sentinel development database. It verifies internal consistency between those records, not the correctness of the extraction against every page of either PDF. The source PDFs and the KICD catalogue must still be checked by a human reviewer before the revised document is marked approved.
